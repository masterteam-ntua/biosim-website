BioSIm's alumna Dr. Asimina Kiourti, an assistant professor in electrical and computer engineering at The Ohio State University, has been named to the prestigious Columbus Business First 40 Under 40 list for 2021.

May 31, 2021

BioSIm’s alumna Dr. Asimina Kiourti, an Assistant Professor in electrical and computer engineering at The Ohio State University, has been announced as a member of “Columbus Business First” 40 Under 40 Class of 2021. This annual award program recognizes Central Ohio’s best and brightest young community leaders who champion people and their potential through partnerships, creating opportunities for communities and future leaders to thrive.

40 Under 40 is the longest-running awards program at Columbus Business First. The competitive selection process included a record number of applications this year, with more than 250 submissions backed by letters of support from esteemed colleagues and collaborators.

Kiourti received her Ph.D. in electrical and computer engineering from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National Technical University of Athens, in 2013 and her Master of Science degree from University College London, UK, in 2009. Since 2016 she is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The Ohio State University where she leads the Wearable and Implantable Technologies group. Kiourti’s team carries out interdisciplinary research at the intersection of bio-electromagnetics, wearable and implantable antennas, sensors for body area applications, and flexible textile-based electronics.
