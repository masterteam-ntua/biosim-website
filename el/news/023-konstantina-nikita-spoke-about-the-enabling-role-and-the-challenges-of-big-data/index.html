Konstantina Nikita spoke about the enabling role and the challenges of big data in healthcare at the Delphi Economic Forum, that took place on April 6-9, 2022

April 11, 2022

Prof. Konstantina Nikita was a speaker at the Delphi Economic Forum, that took place on April 6-9, 2022. She participated in two events organized by the Onassis Cardiac Surgery Center, that figured as a content partner of this year’s forum. Both events aimed at shedding light on aspects shaping the future of transplantation worldwide through innovation and technological developments while also highlighting the opportunities and challenges of technological advances and big data for transforming healthcare. Prof. Nikita discussed the enabling role of big data and went through the main challenges that have to be addressed towards unlocking the full potential of big data in reshaping the landscape of healthcare.
