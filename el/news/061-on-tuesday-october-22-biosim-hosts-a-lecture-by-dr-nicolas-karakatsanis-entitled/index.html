On Tuesday, October 22, BioSim hosts a lecture by Dr Nicolas Karakatsanis entitled “Quantitative and cost-effective Positron Emission Tomography: towards multi-parametric molecular imaging and utilization of sparsely-distributed detectors configuration"

October 21, 2019

On Tuesday, October 22, Nicolas (Nikolaos) A. Karakatsanis, Assistant Professor in Biomedical Engineering at the Department of Radiology at Weill Cornell Medicine, Cornell University in New York, will give a lecture entitled “Quantitative and cost-effective Positron Emission Tomography: towards multi-parametric molecular imaging and utilization of sparsely-distributed detectors configurations” at 14:00 at the Multimedia Amphitheater of the National Technical University of Athens Central Library. The event is organised by BioSim Laboratory of the School of ECE, NTUA with the support of IEEE Greece Section, IEEE NTUA EMB Chapter and IEEE NTUA Student Branch.
