On May 31, 2021, 12:00 CEST, Konstantina Nikita will deliver a speech titled "Harnessing AI to deliver a personalised and efficient health and wellness journey" in the upcoming [31st Medical Informatics Europe (MIE) Conference](https://mie2021.vfairs.com/en/), that will take place on 29th - 31st May 2021, from 9:00 to 18:30 CEST.

The conference programme is available [here](https://mie2021.vfairs.com/en/#agenda).
