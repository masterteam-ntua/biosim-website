Professor Nantia Nikita presents current research activities at the Biomedical Simulations and Imaging Lab of the School of ECE, NTUA. Wireless, wearable, and ambient technologies are combined with simulation techniques and advanced data analytics, harnessing Explainable Artificial Intelligence, towards the enhanced understanding of pathophysiological mechanisms, with the ultimate goal to facilitate self-disease management and assist tailored therapeutic interventions.
