NTUA BioΤech Day 2021 brings together students from the courses “e-Health and m-Health Technologies” of the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, NTUA and “Biodesign” of the School of Mechanical Engineering, to pitch their projects to a panel of experts who will evaluate them in terms of technological innovation and social impact. The event will take place on July 6, at 1 p.m., via Webex. You can find the program and details of the event here.
