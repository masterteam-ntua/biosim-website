On July 25, 2023, Konstantina Nikita delivered a keynote talk on “Building divergent career paths based on convergence principles” during the Women in Engineering and Radio Science at the IEEE International Symposium on Antennas and Propagation and USNC-URSI Radio Science Meeting in Portland, USA. The talk was followed by a 40-minute panel in an interactive format with the audience that focused on career, mentorship, personal challenges/experiences, work-life balance, and more. For this panel, Konstantina Nikita was joined by Prof. Jennifer Bernhard (University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, USA), Prof. Cynthia Furse (University of Utah, USA), Prof. Mahta Moghaddam (University of Southern California, USA), and Dr. Jeanne Quimby (National Institute of Standards and Technology, USA).

The session’s program is available [here](https://2023.apsursi.org/wie.php).
