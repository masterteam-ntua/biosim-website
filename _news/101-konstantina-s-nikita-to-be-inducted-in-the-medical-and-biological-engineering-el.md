Τhe American Institute for Medical and Biological Engineering (AIMBE) has announced the pending induction of Konstantina S. Nikita, Ph.D., Ph.D., M.D., Full Professor, School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, National Technical University of Athens, to its College of Fellows. Dr. Nikita was nominated, reviewed, and elected by peers and members of the College of Fellows for contributions to modeling bioelectromagnetic interactions and biomedical imaging.
