Biotech Days were successfully completed on the 1st of June. NTUA alumni, George Varsos, Omiros Exarhos, Asimina Glinou, Giannis Stoitsis and Despoina Moschou gave inspiring talks in a series of events that took place in May. The speakers shared their journey in the field of biomedical engineering and engaged in a lively discussion with the attending students about the founding process and the growth potential of a biotech start-up, issues related to innovation and intellectual property, as well as ground-breaking innovative biomedical technologies like Lab-on-Chip.
