Positions for software engineers are open at the Center for AI and Data Science for Integrated Diagnostics (AI2D) at the University of Pennsylvania. These positions will be remote (stationed in Greece) and will be handled via a collaboration between AI2D and NTUA's BioSim laboratory. The software engineers will be involved in projects related to a program funded by the Brain Initiative of the US NIH, and which relates to application of machine learning methods to imaging data from over 65,000 individuals and 25 different studies, in order to create a web-accessible brain chart. Collaborations with Amazon Web Services have facilitated the first prototype of this system: https://neuroimagingchart.com/
Knowledge of Python, machine learning algorithms and web-based computing are important. Salary will be in the range of 1,500 to 3,300 euros per month, depending on qualifications. You can apply via email to the following address: knikita@ece.ntua.gr
