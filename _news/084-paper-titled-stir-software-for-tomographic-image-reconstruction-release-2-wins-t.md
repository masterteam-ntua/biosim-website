BioSim's graduates Charalampos Tsoumpas and Nikolaos Dikaios have won the 2017 Physics in Medicine & Biology (PMB) citations prize as co-authors of the paper "STIR: software for tomographic image reconstruction release 2", which has been published in the Physics in Medicine & Biology journal. Find out more [here](http://medicalphysicsweb.org/cws/article/research/70630).
