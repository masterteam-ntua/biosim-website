On January 29, 2020, BioSim had the pleasure of hosting at its premises Andreas Andreou, Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. Professor Andreou made an interesting presentation on research activities and ongoing projects at his lab. Fruitful discussions followed, where BioSim members and Professor Andreou had the opportunity of sharing their personal research interests and spoke about emerging trends in biomedical engineering while exploring room for potential collaborations.
