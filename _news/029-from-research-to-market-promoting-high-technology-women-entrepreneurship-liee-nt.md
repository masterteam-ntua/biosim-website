On Oct 25, 2021, Konstantina Nikita and Konstantia Zarkogianni presented their lectures during the online workshop entitled “From research to market: promoting high-technology women entrepreneurship”, which was organized by the Laboratory of Industrial and Energy Economics at the National Technical University of Athens (LIEE NTUA) in collaboration with the Incubator EPI.noo and the Invent ICT Program.

Prof. Konstantina Nikita presented her speech on “From the Lab to the marketplace: Leading innovation” during the “Valorization of Research Results” session of the conference. The video lecture is available here (timestamps, 17:00 - 34:50).

Dr. Konstantia Zarkogianni presented her speech on “Nous4Health” during the “Start-up Technology Entrepreneurship” session of the conference. Her video lecture is available here (timestamps, 1:05:15 - 1:17:49).

The LIEE NTUA in collaboration with the Incubator EPI.noo and the Invent ICT Program, organised for the third consecutive time this very successful workshop dedicated on women entrepreneurship, in the context of the global initiative “Women Entrepreneurship Week 2021”. This year’s workshop hosted women from academia with a solid research presence who are simultaneously interested in exploiting the results of their research, but also young women with a strong research profile who are trying to turn their research into specific knowledge and technology-intensive products and services. The workshop was coordinated by Dr. Aimilia Protogerou, Senior Researcher at the Laboratory of Industrial and Energy Economics (NTUA).
