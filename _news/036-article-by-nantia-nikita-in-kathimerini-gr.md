An article by Nantia Nikita titled "The contribution of 5G technologies in healthcare during the pandemic and the roads that open up for the future" has been published in the greek newspaper kathimerini.gr. The article describes how 5G networks' performance advancements will serve as the backbone of emerging technologies that can be harnessed in order to transform delivery of care. The article can be found [here](https://www.kathimerini.gr/life/technology/561376816/pos-i-technologia-esose-zoes-sti-diarkeia-tis-pandimias/).
