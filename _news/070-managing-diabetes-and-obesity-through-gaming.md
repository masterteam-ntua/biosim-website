An article about BioSim, titled "Managing diabetes and obesity through gaming", has been published in the greek newspaper [taNea.gr](https://www.tanea.gr). The article features BioSim's research activities on the design and development of innovative personalized health systems that facilitate diabetes and obesity self-management, by leveraging advanced modeling techniques along with novel gamification mechanisms. The article can be found [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pij0LXs2CK_cHDyq0Fdl8SkQ5RbMkOAZ/view?usp=sharing).
