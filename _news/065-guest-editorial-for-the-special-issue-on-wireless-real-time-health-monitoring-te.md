Professor Nantia Nikita is guest co-editor of the upcoming Special Issue of the IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation on **Wireless Real-time Health Monitoring Technology for Personalized Medicine**. The Guest Editorial for the Special Issue, co-authored by Gianluca Lazzi, Raphael Lee, and Nantia Nikita, has just been published and is available [here](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/8794687/authors#authors).
