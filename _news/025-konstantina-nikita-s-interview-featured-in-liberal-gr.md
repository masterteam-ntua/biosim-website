Konstantina Nikita gave an interview in the Greek newspaper Liberal.gr, titled “Artificial Intelligence and Greece in the battle against the pandemic”. In this interview Professor Nikita, explains the role of Artificial Intelligence in the battle against the pandemic and presents smarty4covid research activities on discovering novel biomarkers with high prognostic value regarding the onset and the progress of COVID-19.
