BioSim launches "smarty4covid", a new research project dedicated to the development of an Intelligent Multimodal Framework for COVID-19 Risk Assessment and Monitoring based on Explainable Deep Learning. The project is funded by the [Hellenic Foundation for Research and Innovation](https://www.elidek.gr/). BioSim will partner with [AILS lab](https://www.ails.ece.ntua.gr/) towards the discovery of novel biomarkers of infection and disease progression by applying multimodal deep learning techniques on heterogeneous data consisting of self-reported risk factors, along with audio recordings of breathing, voice and speech, with the aim to:

- increase the sensitivity of the screening procedure by detecting cases with high risk of COVID-19 infection,
- enable the remote monitoring of patients with COVID-19, and
- provide alert signals in case of emergency.
