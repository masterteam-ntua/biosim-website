Mitsis Konstantinos obtained the Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens in 2013. He is currently working towards the Ph.D. degree in the BIOSIM Laboratory, SECE, NTUA. He has authored one research paper that was presented in international conference and two papers that were published in international journals.

**Research Interests**
Machine Learning, Physiological Systems Modeling, Medical Decision Support Systems, Diabetes Mellitus Management, Gamification Mechanisms, Serious Games and Health Applications Development
