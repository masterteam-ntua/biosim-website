The increasing availability of multimodal biomedical data, including medical imaging, physiological signals and clinical records, creates new opportunities for more comprehensive and personalized healthcare applications. At the same time, differences across data modalities, patient populations, acquisition devices and clinical centers can significantly affect the reliability and generalization of AI models. Our research focuses on the development of resilient, interpretable and multimodal AI methods, with emphasis on data fusion, domain adaptation, uncertainty estimation and explainability, aiming to support reliable and clinically meaningful predictions under changing real-world conditions.
