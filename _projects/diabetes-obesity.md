BioSim deals with data-driven and interpretable methodologies for the prevention, monitoring, and personalized management of diabetes and obesity. Research in this area integrates continuous glucose monitoring, clinical information, physiological signals, lifestyle factors, and behavioral data to support the prediction of hypoglycemia and diabetes-related complications, automated detection of clinically relevant events, and personalized risk stratification.

These activities are complemented by intelligent digital health interventions, including mobile and wearable technologies, serious games, gamification, biofeedback, and adaptive decision-support tools. The overall objective is to promote healthier behaviors, patient engagement, long-term self-management, and more proactive and personalized management of diabetes and obesity.
