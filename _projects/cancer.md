BioSim studies artificial intelligence and multimodal data-analysis methodologies for improved cancer characterization and clinical decision support. Research focuses on the integration of histopathological, omics, and medical imaging data for disease subtyping, survival prediction, biomarker identification, and the characterization of treatment-related changes.

Particular emphasis is placed on computational approaches for distinguishing true disease progression from pseudoprogression using MRI, supporting more accurate assessment of tumour evolution and improved personalization of patient management.
