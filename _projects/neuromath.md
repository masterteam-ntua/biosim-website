NEUROMATH aimed to increase knowledge on mathematical methods for estimating cortical activity and connectivity in the human brain. The action supported multimodal integration of neuroelectromagnetic and hemodynamic measurements and applications ranging from cognitive tasks to brain-computer interfaces.
