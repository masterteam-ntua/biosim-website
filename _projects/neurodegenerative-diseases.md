BioSim develops multimodal and AI-driven approaches for the characterization of brain ageing, cognitive decline, and neurodegenerative disease heterogeneity. Research integrates structural and functional neuroimaging, diffusion imaging, brain connectivity, metabolomic data, physiological and behavioral information, and cognitive measurements to identify biomarkers and disease signatures associated with neurodegeneration and its progression.

Machine learning, deep learning, graph-based modelling, and unsupervised learning are employed to identify clinically meaningful subgroups and uncover complex patterns of brain heterogeneity. Particular emphasis is placed on the detection of subtle preclinical changes, longitudinal modelling, robust biomarker discovery, multimodal integration, and the development of interpretable and bias-aware models that generalize across populations and acquisition environments.

Research in this area also investigates objective assessment of cognitive state through EEG and complementary physiological and behavioral signals. Such multimodal approaches support the characterization of cognitive load, attention, fatigue, and related processes and contribute to the development of adaptive systems capable of responding intelligently to the cognitive state of the user.
