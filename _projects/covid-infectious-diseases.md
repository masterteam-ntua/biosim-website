BioSim develops data-driven approaches for the detection, monitoring, and modelling of infectious diseases, with emphasis on multimodal physiological data and explainable artificial intelligence. Research has included the analysis of physiological audio signals for disease detection, interpretable knowledge representation, and modelling of disease progression.

These activities combine machine learning with multimodal data analysis and robust, explainable methodologies, aiming to support reliable risk assessment and clinically meaningful decision support under changing real-world conditions.
