Smart health systems integrate sensing technologies, physiological and behavioral data, artificial intelligence, and interactive digital tools to support continuous, personalized, and data-driven healthcare. Our research focuses on the design and development of intelligent and adaptive systems that combine wearable and mobile technologies, biosignal processing, machine learning, serious games, and decision-support mechanisms. By capturing and interpreting information about the user's physiological, behavioral, and contextual state, these systems can provide personalized feedback, promote engagement and self-management, and dynamically adapt their operation to individual needs. Our goal is to develop reliable and user-centered digital health solutions for remote monitoring, prevention, rehabilitation, and personalized intervention, bridging biomedical engineering, artificial intelligence, and human-computer interaction.
