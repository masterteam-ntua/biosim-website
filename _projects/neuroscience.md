The increasing prevalence of cognitive disorders, which can range from attention deficits and mental fatigue to stress-induced performance decline, underscores the need for objective and real-time assessment of human cognitive states. Electroencephalography (EEG), due to its characteristics (high temporal resolution, etc.) and its ability to record neural electrical activity, constitutes a particularly powerful tool for monitoring cognitive load, attention processes, and fatigue. Moreover, combining EEG-derived features with other data (physiological, behavioral, etc.) enables the development of multimodal frameworks for assessing cognitive state in interactive environments, human-computer interaction applications, adaptive systems, simulation environments, and serious games. Through this multidisciplinary approach, our goal is to advance the understanding of cognition and to contribute to the design of systems that intelligently adapt to the user's cognitive state.
