Eleftherios Kalafatis obtained the Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens in 2020 with a GPA of 8.72/10. He is currently working towards the Ph.D. degree in the BIOSIM Laboratory, SECE, NTUA. He has authored one research paper that was presented in international conference and one in a national conference.

Research Interests Machine Learning, Physiological Systems Modeling, Medical Decision Support Systems, Diabetes Mellitus Management, Gamification Mechanisms, Serious Games and Health Applications Development
