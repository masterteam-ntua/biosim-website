Denys Nikolayev

Visitor

Email

Denys Nikolayev was born on 20/09/1986 in Lviv, Ukraine. In November 2008, he received the MSc degree in electronics and telecommunications at the Lviv Polytechnic National University. In September 2012 he started the PhD degree studies at the University of West Bohemia in Pilsen (Czech Republic) and in 2013 the joint supervision PhD studies with the Institute of Electronics and Telecommunication of Rennes (France).

Research Interests Simulation of Electromagnetic Fields and Coupled Problems, Wireless Biomedical Telemetry, Miniature Microstrip Antennas and Bioelectromagnetics

Scientific Professional Societies Bioelectromagnetics Society (BEMS), European BioElectromagnetics Association (EBEA)
