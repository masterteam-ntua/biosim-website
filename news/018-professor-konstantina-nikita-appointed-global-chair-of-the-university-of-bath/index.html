Professor Konstantina Nikita appointed Global Chair of the University of Bath

September 22, 2022

Professor Konstantina Nikita has been appointed Global Chair of the University of Bath for the academic year 2022-23. She is among the four distinguished international visiting professors that will be joining Bath to enhance its strategic research network with universities and research institutes overseas.

Professor Nikita will be hosted by the Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering to form multiple research collaborations in at least three of Bath’s key research centres and participate in a number of key research projects in the areas of health and digitalisation, including: AI-assisted multisensory inputs for augmenting physical performance, subcutaneous implantable sensing device technology and artificial retinal devices for augmented human vision. She will also engage with the Faculty Women in Engineering Society and the Vertically Integrated Project (VIP) Team Bath Biodevices without Borders.

Bath’s Global Chair scheme invites distinguished international visiting professors and builds high-profile partnerships in research and practice. Global Chairs contribute to environmental sustainability, climate action and water security, with research on new materials and technologies, biodiversity, governance and policy.
