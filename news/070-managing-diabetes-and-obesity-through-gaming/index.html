Managing diabetes and obesity through gaming

March 5, 2019

An article about BioSim, titled “Managing diabetes and obesity through gaming”, has been published in the greek newspaper taNea.gr. The article features BioSim’s research activities on the design and development of innovative personalized health systems that facilitate diabetes and obesity self-management, by leveraging advanced modeling techniques along with novel gamification mechanisms.
